# Aeryvex Pro — player-driven release roadmap
These are development targets, NOT finished features.

## Combat and identity
- Distinct movesets, stamina tuning, melee impact audio, hit-stop and readable telegraphs.
- Original commercial-use sci-fi characters, costumes, voices, music, and locations.
- AI difficulty playtesting and controller accessibility.

## Battlefront-style large battles
- Squad/class selection (assault, support, specialist), friendly and hostile team AI.
- Capture and hold command points, tickets, respawn flow, match summary.
- Tactical maps, vehicle/drivable-vehicle prototype, scalable troop count and LOD.
- Galactic Conquest campaign map with capture territories and persistent progression.

## Campaign and retention
- Complete short original campaign with objectives, save slots, checkpoints, bosses, and ending.
- Character customization and unlocks earned through play.
- Settings, input remapping, audio, subtitles, accessibility, and PC exports.

## Release gates
- All modes have start, win, lose, retry, and exit pathways.
- Engine opens project without parser/runtime errors; tested on target Linux hardware.
- Measured performance, file-size budget, playtests, licensing audit, storefront assets.
