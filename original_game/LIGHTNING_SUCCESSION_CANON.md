# Aeryvex Pro — Canon: Lightning Combat and Final Defeat

## Core rule: battle victory versus complete destruction
A warrior can win a duel without destroying the opponent's lightning essence. A defeated fighter may survive, be captured, or die and pass power to a successor. **Complete destruction** means the enemy's essence is extinguished and no lightning power transfers.

## Aether ranks
- **Rainbow Grand Master (Orion Valen):** leader, chooses a successor and transfers rainbow power through the Rite of Seven Currents, then persists as a Rainbow Spirit. Can cast rainbow lightning from staff and hands and transform into an unstoppable rainbow-lightning body. The Grand Master has the unique strength needed to stop the **supreme Void Sovereign Lord** from transferring power to the chosen successor upon final defeat.
- **Purple Masters:** sit on the Aether Council, direct policy, and lead armies in battle. They can defeat and **completely destroy many Void Lords**, preventing those Lords' red power from transferring. They may fight the supreme Void Sovereign Lord, but final nullification of that supreme Lord's inheritance requires the Rainbow Grand Master.
- **Blue Warriors:** regular Aether army. Can permanently destroy opposing **regular Void warriors**, preventing ordinary dark-warrior power transfer. They cannot normally completely destroy a Void Lord.
- **Yellow Temple Guards:** specialized sanctuary defenders with yellow lightning; can fight and fully defeat threats appropriate to their comparable rank.
- All users can cast lightning from hands at full focus. Weapons project actual branching, straight or curved lightning, not rigid swords.

## Void ranks
- **Void Sovereign Lord (Drakthar Vex):** leader and supreme red-lightning wielder, with full-body Crimson Cataclysm transformation. Unless his essence is nullified by an ascended Rainbow Grand Master, defeat/death can transmit supreme power to his chosen successor.
- **Void Lords:** elite generals and regional rulers. When slain but not completely destroyed, each sends their power to the individual designated beneath them. Several fallen Lords can transfer power to different inheritors. A Purple Master can completely destroy these Lords and stop their transfers.
- **Regular Void warriors:** may use red lightning from hands or weapons. A Blue Aether warrior can completely destroy one, canceling that warrior's transfer.
- **Symmetry:** comparable Void combatants can permanently destroy equivalently ranked Aether opponents under the same rules. A higher-ranked Aether fighter who dies without complete destruction may still have an inheritable current, subject to the Aether succession rules. Grand Master succession is unique and chosen while living, with a rainbow spirit afterward.

## Story and gameplay
- Rank sets who can nullify an opponent's transferable essence, **not** who can win any individual fight. A clever lower-ranking fighter can outfight a stronger enemy, but that does not automatically extinguish the higher-ranked essence.
- "Only the Grand Master can stop the supreme Void Sovereign Lord from transferring power" refers specifically to complete nullification of his supreme inheritance; it does **not** mean Purple Masters cannot defeat lesser Void Lords.
- Before a boss fight, show **Defeated** vs **Essence Destroyed** vs **Power Transferred** as separate outcomes.
- When a named Lord transfers power, the chosen heir gains new combat potential and political influence in later chapters.
- Final design choices about exact rank matchups and rare succession exceptions require player approval, not assumed implementation.
