# The Prism Council of Four — Aeryvex Pro story canon v2

## The four Aether Grand Masters
The Aether Order is led by **four living Grand Masters**, collectively the **Prism Council**. They are peers on questions of war and doctrine, though the First Grand Master chairs the council. All four can channel rainbow lightning through hands or focus staffs and achieve a living rainbow-lightning-body state called **Rainbow Ascension**. Each is distinct in personality, approach to war, and combat specialty.

1. **Orion Valen — First Grand Master, the Shield of Worlds.** White-haired elder wearing navy-and-gold ceremonial armor. A compassionate but politically burdened leader; his rainbow storm favors immense protective fields and counterattacks. He broke with his former companion Drakthar over whether peace justifies coercion.
2. **Maeryn Sol — Grand Master of the Living Gates.** Dark-skinned woman with close-cropped silver curls, layered teal-and-copper robes, asymmetrical spiral staff and distinct bronze portal-ring jewelry. Controls the geometry and stability of Celestial Arches and can weave branching rainbow lightning into protective gates. Fears the council has become overly militarized.
3. **Thalen Orris — Grand Master of the Marching Storm.** Broad-shouldered nonhuman with indigo patterned skin and three ridged brows, pale braided crest, segmented sandstone armor and a forked staff. Leads relief campaigns and frontline defenses. In Ascension, his rainbow body produces jagged moving storm-front forms. Wants the Order to defend every threatened system rather than negotiate.
4. **Ilyra Venn — Grand Master of Hidden Currents.** Tall woman with copper skin, black-and-white braided hair, dark pearl and plum robes, five hovering conductor-rings around her staff. Commands intelligence, counter-espionage, and investigations of corruption. Her Rainbow Ascension splits into rapidly reconnecting electrical afterimages. Suspects that a member of the Prism Council enabled the first portal siege.

All four form the highest governing council. **Purple Masters** form the wider **Conclave of Masters**, serving directly under the four Grand Masters as lawmakers, judges, and battlefield generals; purple Masters can permanently destroy many lesser Void Lords. Blue Warriors are the main army; Yellow Temple Guards defend sacred locations. All ranks can emit their rank-colored lightning from hands as well as their focus weapons.

## The fallen fifth — Drakthar Vex
Long before the story begins, **Drakthar Vex was the fifth Aether Grand Master**—the celebrated **Grand Master of Unbroken Order**. He led the battle that reopened the first Celestial Arch and claimed that independent kingdoms would keep destroying one another unless the portals were united under permanent military command.

He secretly experimented with coercively binding the wills of defeated enemies through their inherited lightning. When the other Grand Masters uncovered the experiments, Drakthar rejected their ruling. At the **Sundering of the Prism**, he shattered his rainbow current into a malignant crimson frequency, permanently renouncing the Aether succession rite and declaring himself the **Void Sovereign Lord**.

His defection left **four** Aether Grand Masters. The Void Dominion tells a rival history: Drakthar was betrayed by a council unwilling to do what was necessary to stop the wars.

At the heart of the campaign, the player finds evidence that some of Drakthar's accusations were true, though his methods remain brutal. His former allies know how he fights, and he knows their fears. This supports meaningful dialogue, espionage missions, council disputes, and personal boss duels rather than a simple good-versus-evil retelling of another franchise.

## Lightning-body and power-transfer rules
- Each Grand Master can fire **rainbow lightning** from their hands and staffs and enter **Rainbow Ascension**, a full humanoid made of rainbow electricity. The form is mythically overwhelming against ordinary armies, serves as a battle-ending transformation, and has gameplay counterplay in major boss encounters.
- Drakthar can emit **red lightning** through hands and focus weapon, and become a full crimson-lightning body in **Crimson Cataclysm**.
- A Purple Master can beat and **completely destroy many ordinary Void Lords**, so that their essence cannot transfer.
- Blue Warriors can completely destroy regular Void Warriors; Void combatants of equivalent power can do likewise to Aether enemies.
- Any one of the **four living Rainbow Grand Masters** can, when sufficiently empowered and victorious, **completely extinguish Drakthar's supreme crimson inheritance**. Lesser ranks may win exchanges or force retreats but cannot nullify that supreme power transfer. The final story may require cooperation among several Grand Masters, or the player's ascent to the office.
- Each **living Grand Master chooses their own successor**. The Rite of Seven Currents transfers that Grand Master's rainbow current to the chosen heir; the predecessor becomes a **Rainbow Spirit**. This preserves four living seats; if a Grand Master has not completed succession, the seat is vacant and its power is not automatically inherited.
- On the Void side, each Lord designates a subordinate who inherits that Lord's crimson power upon death **unless the Lord's essence was completely destroyed**. The supreme Void Sovereign Lord similarly designates an heir, unless nullified by an Aether Grand Master.
- A candidate becoming a Grand Master requires the chosen Grand Master's actual transfer; a Purple Master cannot simply take the title through a vote or duel.

## Campaign hooks
**Chapter 1 — Siege of Kharos:** The player's mercy or duty decision influences which Grand Masters trust them.
**Council of Four:** The player can petition all four, witness votes about conquest versus protection, uncover secrets and gain differing mentors.
**Ghost of the Fifth:** Drakthar's pre-fall records reveal why the Council became four. Some were falsified.
**The Last Inheritance:** Drakthar reveals the name of his successor. Victory alone may strengthen the next Sovereign unless a living Grand Master extinguishes the crimson current.
**Four Storms:** The four Grand Masters enter Rainbow Ascension to hold multiple invaded portal fronts while the player confronts the central antagonist.
**Endings:** defend independent galaxies; establish negotiated gate governance; reform the Order; seize political control; or reject both factions and build a new coalition. Different survival and succession states persist.

## Design originality
Original named characters, intergalactic portal warfare, rank-colored lightning systems, conflicting council philosophies, and legacy-transfer mechanics. Avoid recognizable copyrighted costumes, iconic characters, specific franchise plot recreations, insignia, and trademark-confusing branding. Names and looks remain subject to pre-release clearance.
