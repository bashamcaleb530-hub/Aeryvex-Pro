# Aeryvex Pro — Original Portal War prototype

This is a **new, standalone Godot 4 prototype**, not a replacement for existing Godot 3/4 arena content. Open **this subfolder** in Godot 4, run project and use WASD/arrows to move; Space attacks, Shift dodges and E interacts with the portal. On a choice screen press **1** to save your friend or **2** to protect the royal family. Win the commander duel to change who controls Kharos. Press R to restart.

**What works in the prototype:** walkable portal, enemy wave, lightning-arc attack, health, consequential decision, leader duel and victory state. The prototype uses colored drawn shapes and lightning lines as temporary test visuals. **Realistic textured characters, skeletal animations, cinematic environments, all ten modes and complete campaigns are NOT implemented.**

Original roster and art production rules: `aeryvex_game_design.json`.
No Star Wars names, models or licensed reference images are imported.
