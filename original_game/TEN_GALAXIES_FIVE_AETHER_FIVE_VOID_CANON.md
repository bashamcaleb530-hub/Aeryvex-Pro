# Aeryvex Pro — Ten Galaxies, Ten Supreme Rulers (Canon v3)

## The ten-galaxy order
There are **ten major playable galaxies** divided into two rival blocs, connected by the ancient walk-through **Celestial Arches**. Intergalactic travel, invasion and espionage operate through portals, not pilotable starships.

### Five Aether galaxies — Council of Five Grand Masters
The **Prism Council of Five** has one Grand Master governing and defending each Aether galaxy. Orion Valen is **First Grand Master**, presides over all five and leads the Aether alliance; other four Grand Masters retain responsibility for their own galaxies, military campaigns, councils and subjects. All five can achieve Rainbow Ascension and may extinguish a supreme Void inheritance when fully empowered.

1. **Aurelia — Orion Valen, First Grand Master.** Capital: **Crownspire of Lumen**. Council headquarters, archives, rainbow succession sanctum, diplomatic center. Orion directs joint defense of all five galaxies. A respected but fallible elder; his closest former friend was Drakthar Vex.
2. **Velithra — Maeryn Sol, Grand Master of the Living Gates.** Capital: **Halcyon Arch**. Oceanic cities, portal engineers, bridge-citadels and trade. Specializes in portal control, civilian evacuation and dimensional barriers.
3. **Ormath — Thalen Orris, Grand Master of the Marching Storm.** Capital: **Stonewake Bastion**. Mountains, fortress world-rings, military academies and ground armies. Specializes in frontline defense, infantry command and shockwave storms.
4. **Eidara — Ilyra Venn, Grand Master of Hidden Currents.** Capital: **Mirrorhall Enclave**. Dense vertical cities and hidden corridors; intelligence, counterespionage, investigation and secret portal reconnaissance.
5. **Cyravelle — Selene Avaris, Grand Master of the Living Chorus.** Capital: **Sunweave Terraces**. Bioluminescent habitats and ancient healing sanctuaries. Original new fifth Grand Master: mature woman with deep bronze skin, tightly coiled white hair, layered jade-and-ivory woven coat, geometric copper halo conductors (not a literal crown), and a branching rainbow-electric ribbon emitted from hands or a tuning staff. Expert in restoration, calming unstable lightning currents, saving populations and severing harmful transfer rituals.

**Aether ranks:** Five Rainbow Grand Masters jointly form the supreme Prism Council; **Purple Masters** serve on subordinate Conclave councils and lead battlefront armies; **Blue Warriors** form the regular armies; **Yellow Temple Guards** defend portal sanctuaries and sacred sites. All can cast their color lightning from hands, and at high power their specialized weapons. Lightning is free electricity in straight, branched, curved arcs—not a solid sword blade.

### Five Void galaxies — Council of Five Void Grand Lords
The Void Dominion commands **five dark galaxies**, each ruled by its own **Void Grand Lord**, with Drakthar Vex as the **High Void Sovereign Lord**, the first among the five and the supreme dark ruler. The other four dark grand lords maintain military and political control over their respective galaxies. Unlike the Aether Council, their alliance is held together by dangerous inheritance pacts and contested loyalty.

6. **Nexavar — Drakthar Vex, High Void Sovereign Lord.** Capital: **Crimson Throne of Voss**. Formerly the FIFTH Aether Grand Master before his rebellion (the Council later restored five living seats). Has a full crimson-lightning body transformation, **Crimson Cataclysm**, and can channel red lightning through weapon, hands and body. He seeks to control all Celestial Arches.
7. **Kharvoss — Malveris Thorne, Void Grand Lord of the Iron Host.** Capital: **Dreadforge Citadel**. Obsidian factories, siege legions and fortress gates. Broad plated warlord with copper-black war armor and asymmetrical exposed coil channels. Red lightning: heavy thunder impacts.
8. **Veyscar — Nyssara Veil, Void Grand Lady of the Veiled Court.** Capital: **Nocturne Spire**. Shadow market cities, spies, political blackmail and assassins. Tall pale-blue alien with layered glass-like black garments and cascading wire filaments. Red lightning: deceptive feints and concealed arcs.
9. **Urakhen — Korr Uldane, Void Grand Lord of the Wild Furnace.** Capital: **Ashfall Crown**. Molten volcanic chasms and raider fortresses. Huge four-armed nonhuman with charcoal-scaled skin and ceramic armor; red lightning: chain strikes and area devastation.
10. **Mournreach — Vaelith Sorn, Void Grand Lady of the Broken Choir.** Capital: **Hollow Requiem**. Crystalline grave-cities and inheritance shrines. Lean elderly woman with dark iridescent skin, fractured white porcelain mask and silver-black conductor rings. Red lightning: inherited echoes, suppression and soul-binding at the edge of legality in the game's universe.

### Shared inheritance rules and the fallen Grand Master
- **Drakthar Vex was a former Aether Grand Master**, then fell and founded the Void Dominion. His fifth seat was ultimately filled, restoring the current Aether Council of Five.
- **Aether:** Each living Grand Master selects their successor and transfers rainbow current through the Rite of Seven Currents, becoming a conscious Rainbow Spirit. Five living seats continue through deliberate transfer, not public elections.
- **Void:** Each Void Grand Lord designates an heir beneath them. If the Lord dies without the lightning essence being completely destroyed, that Lord's inherited red current transfers to their appointed successor. The five may select different heirs. Drakthar's supreme succession works the same way but can only be conclusively nullified by an empowered Aether Grand Master.
- Purple Aether Masters can completely destroy many lesser Void Lords and prevent transfer. Blue warriors can completely destroy regular Void warriors. The same rank-based rules broadly apply in reverse: Void combatants of comparable power can extinguish Aether essences. A defeat, a death with transfer, and complete destruction must be recorded as separate battle outcomes.
- A Void Grand Lord is more powerful than a common Void Lord, and defeating one does not automatically stop their inheritance. How each of the other four grand lords' power can be permanently severed is a boss mechanic to develop, consistent with rank-based capabilities.

## Campaign engine
**Act I: A Gate Bleeds** — the Void attacks a portal at the border of Aurelia and Kharvoss. Choices decide whether to save a friend or defend a royal household.
**Act II: The Five Councils** — the player travels to the five Aether galaxies, builds trust with the five Grand Masters and learns their disagreements.
**Act III: The Five Dark Thrones** — espionage, portal sieges and boss confrontations in five Void galaxies, including the four competing Void Grand Lords.
**Act IV: Inheritance War** — slain commanders pass red current to heirs unless completely destroyed. Control of portals shifts and remembered decisions affect armies and succession.
**Act V: The Fallen Fifth** — Drakthar reveals what happened when he was an Aether Grand Master. The player may fight for peace, conquer, reform the council or attempt a different government.
**Finale: The Ten-Galaxy Crown** — determine who governs the gates; defeating Drakthar does not end the threat unless the player ensures the supreme crimson current cannot pass to a new tyrant.
